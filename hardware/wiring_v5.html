Ember wiring, v5

One pulse sensor, three thermistors (two skin, one ambient) and the IMU. The SHT40 and pulse sensor B are gone. The ambient thermistor takes GPIO5 with its own 10k resistor (R3), wired exactly like the skin thermistors.
